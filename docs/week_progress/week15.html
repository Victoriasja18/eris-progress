Glossary

  • TP_calls: eris’s nearest gene on a side that is the right gene –> because the truth only reported one flanks gene, that is why we ignore the others when calculating precision.
  • FN_wrong_side: eris found the gene but reported it on the other side, e.g. downstream instead of upstream. It counts as a miss.
  • FP_wrong_side: The same wrong-side mistake seen from eris’s side: the gene is real but sits on the wrong side. It counts as a false call, so one wrong-side mistake costs both recall and precision.
  • FP_unplaceable: eris reported a gene whose DNA can’t be found in the reference genome, usually a gene cut off at a contig end in a GFA assembly. It can’t match anything, so it counts as a false call.

Wrong side cause both FP and FN.

Main findings

  • eris finds about three quarters of IS–CDS relations on the complete genome; fragmented assemblies cost about 0.12–0.15 F1.
  • Upstream is consistently more accurate than downstream. Almost all misses come from gene calling, not from the graph walk: allowing 20 hops instead of 3 recovers only 0–2 relations per assembly.
  • Often the right gene is found but a wrong gene sits nearer the IS. On the complete genome this happens for 17 of the 85 relations found: either the IS’s own transposase overhangs its end and is reported as a disrupted flank, or pyfgs calls a short ORF (69–234 bp) that RefSeq does not annotate. Counting only eris’s nearest gene, recall drops to 0.58 / 0.38 / 0.35.
  • Disrupted calls are poor, especially on GFAs, partly because of the same transposase overhang.

Precision, recall and F1

Side: recall is over the truth relations; precision is over eris’s nearest flank on each side (per graph branch). A flank on the wrong side counts as a miss on its true side and a false call on the side reported. Distance: per band (overlapping, near 0–150 bp, far), over relations found on their side. Effect: disrupted only; eris TRUNCATED counts as disrupted.

  • Recall = TP / (TP + FN) over the truth relations.
  • Precision = TP_calls / (TP_calls + FP) over eris’s calls; for side and effect these are eris’s nearest flanks, so TP_calls can be lower than TP (when a wrong gene, such as the IS’s own transposase or a short ORF, sits nearer the IS than the true CDS, the true CDS still counts as found for recall, but eris’s call is an FP).
